WHO WE ARE

Your journey starts with us.

Established in 2014, Puerto Princesa of Wonder Services Cooperative Travel and Tours is based in Puerto Princesa City, Palawan, Philippines. We provide organized tour packages and customized travel arrangements for local and international destinations.

We aim to create memorable travel experiences while showcasing the beauty, culture and communities of Palawan.
